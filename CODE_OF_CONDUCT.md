# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and professional project environment.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Keep project discussions focused and useful.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this code of conduct.

For serious or repeated issues, contact the project maintainer privately through the repository's available contact method.
